🐴 HorseBar

Slap your MacBook — it makes a sound.

HorseBar lives in the menu bar. From there you can set how hard a slap needs to be, pick a random or a specific sound, and adjust the volume.

Requirements

  • MacBook with M1 Pro / M1 Max / M2 or newer. Base M1 and Intel Macs don't have the motion sensor.
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

What gets installed

  • HorseBar.app in Applications.
  • A small background service that reads the motion sensor. It needs administrator rights, which is why the installer asks for your password.

To remove everything later: click 🐴 in the menu bar → ⚙︎ → Uninstall HorseBar…
